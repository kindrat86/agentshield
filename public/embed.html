FAQ

Is it really free?
Yes. The widget is free to embed on any site, commercial or personal. No attribution required (but appreciated).
Do you collect data?
No. All computation happens in the browser. Nothing is sent to our servers. No cookies, no tracking, no signup.
Can I customize the colors?
The widget uses CSS custom properties. You can override them by setting --accent, --bg, etc. on the parent page — the widget inherits from its container.
Will it slow down my site?
No. The widget is static HTML with inline CSS and JS. Under 10KB total. It loads asynchronously via the iframe.
