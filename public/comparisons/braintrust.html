AgentShield vs Braintrust: Enforcement vs Evaluation

Braintrust evaluates AI outputs. AgentShield blocks runaway spending. Eval vs enforcement, why you need both for production AI agents.

What Braintrust Does

Braintrust is an AI evaluation platform. It helps teams test prompts, run evals, log traces, and track accuracy over time. It's built for the experimentation and quality-assurance phase of AI development.

What AgentShield Does

AgentShield is a runtime spend-control firewall. It operates during production, not during evaluation. When your agent is live and making real API calls with real money, AgentShield is the safety net.

When to Use Braintrust

  • You are testing prompts and iterating on quality
  • You need systematic AI output evaluation
  • You want trace-level debugging of AI behavior

When to Use AgentShield

  • Your AI agents are in production making real API calls
  • You need hard financial limits, not quality scores
  • You want to prevent $2,800 mistakes, not evaluate them

Use Both Together

Use Braintrust during development to evaluate prompt quality. Deploy with AgentShield in production to enforce spend limits. Different phases, different tools.
