What You Believe vs. What's True

❌ "API rate limits already protect me"
Rate limits cap requests per minute. They don't cap spend per transaction. A single $133 API call passes every rate limit you have, and your agent can make 21 of them in 60 seconds.
Truth: Rate limits throttle speed, not cost. A $133-per-call endpoint is invisible to your rate limiter.
❌ "I set up budget alerts. I'd notice."
Budget alerts are emails. They arrive after the spend happens. At 3 AM, you're asleep. By the time you wake up, the agent has moved on, and your wallet is $2,800 lighter.
Truth: Alerts tell you about the fire after the house burned down. AgentShield stops the match before it strikes.
❌ "I only run a few agents. I can monitor manually."
Today you run 3 agents. Tomorrow it's 10. Next month it's 50 agents across 4 vendors, each making 200 calls/hour. Manual monitoring doesn't scale, and it breaks exactly when you're tired.
Truth: Human monitoring fails at the exact moment you need it most: 3 AM, weekend, holiday, or when you simply forget.

Questions, Answered

Will this slow down my agents?
No. Every evaluation runs in under 1ms. That's faster than a single network round-trip. Your agents won't notice AgentShield is there, until it saves them from a $133 API call.
Do I need to rewrite my agent code?
No. You route transactions through a single endpoint (/v1/transactions/evaluate) before they execute. One line in your HTTP client. That's it.
What if a rule blocks a legitimate transaction?
The rules are yours. You set the limits, thresholds, and allowlists. If something gets blocked that shouldn't, adjust the rule, or add a merchant to the allowlist. You're always in control.
Is this just another monitoring tool?
No. Monitoring tools watch transactions after they happen. AgentShield blocks them before they execute. The API call never fires. That's the difference between a smoke alarm and a fire extinguisher.
Can I just use rate limits and alerts?
You can, and you'll get the same result I did: a $2,800 bill at 3 AM. Rate limits cap speed, not spend. Alerts arrive after the damage. Read the $2,800 story to see exactly how they fail.
